How to play

String a band: drag from one brass peg to another.

Pluck: tap a band to snap it at whatever is touching it.

Slingshot: pull a band back and let go to fling the enemy sitting on it.

Remove a band: tap the scissors, or press and hold the band.

After each wave, choose one upgrade or a new kind of band: Razor Wire makes enemies bleed, Tesla Coil zaps them, Bouncy Jelly knocks them back.

Common questions

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration? On the title screen, tap SOUND, MUSIC or BUZZ. Each one switches separately.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Where the law requires it, a PRIVACY CHOICES button appears on the title screen. It opens Google's consent form again. You can also turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Is my progress saved? Your best wave, best score and settings are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them.
