SnapBack Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

SnapBack is a game. It has no account and no sign-up, and the developer does not run any servers that receive your data. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and this policy explains what that involves.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, and no analytics of our own.
  • Your best scores and settings stay on your iPhone.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads.
  • You choose whether apps may track you. If you say no, ads are still shown but are not personalised from your activity in other apps.
  • We never sell or rent data.

Data stored on your device

SnapBack saves your best wave, best score, number of runs, and your sound, music and vibration settings in a small file on your iPhone. It is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

SnapBack shows a banner on its menus, an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for a second chance. These ads are served by Google's Mobile Ads SDK. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking) and app-specific identifiers,
  • your IP address, used to estimate your general location,
  • which ads you have seen and how you interacted with them, and
  • crash and performance information about the ads SDK.

When you first open SnapBack, you may be asked for consent through Google's consent form (where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), and then by Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change your consent choices from the PRIVACY CHOICES button on the title screen where it appears, and you can change tracking in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

SnapBack is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.
